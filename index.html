Background / 15 Years

Field-tested,
now producing.

My work is grounded in 15 years of human performance, coaching, sales, and business operations — including more than 9,500 coaching sessions delivered, over $1 million in revenue generated, and a ranking among the top 10 of more than 3,000 Equinox coaches. Before moving fully into creative production, I worked directly with clients, developed coaches, led teams, and managed a $25 million business across five locations and more than 1,500 clients, giving me a practical understanding of customer behavior, field execution, and the business realities behind the work.

Today, I apply that operating perspective across creative production, experience design, and technology — building scalable learning and communication experiences across more than 117 locations. Projects move from business need and stakeholder alignment through strategy, scripting, production, post-production, deployment, and iteration. Technology, AI, and automation extend that capability by helping me prototype faster, build connected systems, and scale solutions beyond a single deliverable, contributing to approximately 20% faster production turnaround.

What Differentiates Me

Bridge, not
specialist.

I'm not solely a creative producer or a technology specialist. I understand the business problem, the audience, and the behavior we're trying to influence, then build the content and technology solution to address it. That means operating as a bridge between business, creative, and technology — taking a strategic priority, shaping the narrative, designing the experience, and producing the final deliverable, often covering work that would otherwise require multiple specialists.
